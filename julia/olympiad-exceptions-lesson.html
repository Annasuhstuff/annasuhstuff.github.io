02 Introduction · Why this matters

Why exceptions win olympiads

In the Use of English and Word Formation parts of the ВсОШ, the test writers deliberately choose the irregular case — the one where the normal rule fails. If you only know the rule, you lose exactly those points.

The rule

one book → two books

Everyone gets this.

The exception

one child → two children

This is the point they test.

Our job

learn rule + exception as a pair

Then no trap surprises you.

Part 1 · Nouns & things

Plurals & countability 🔤

Irregular plurals, foreign endings, and the nouns that look plural but aren't (and vice versa).

05 Nouns · Irregular plurals

Plurals that break the +s rule

Vowel change & -en

  • man → men · woman → women
  • child → children · person → people
  • foot → feet · tooth → teeth
  • mouse → mice · goose → geese
  • ox → oxen

No change (same form)

  • sheep · deer · fish · aircraft
  • series · species · means
  • one sheep → ten sheep

-f / -fe → -ves

  • knife → knives · life → lives
  • wolf → wolves · leaf → leaves
  • wife, half, shelf, thief, loaf

Foreign endings

  • crisis → crises · analysis → analyses
  • phenomenon → phenomena · criterion → criteria
  • datum → data · medium → media
  • cactus → cacti · nucleus → nuclei
⚠️ Exceptions to the -ves rule → these just add -s: roofs, chiefs, cliffs, beliefs, chefs, proofs.   And -o: potatoes/tomatoes/heroes but photos, pianos, kilos, zoos.

07 Nouns · Countable or not?

Nouns that fool you

Uncountable — always singular

no plural, no a/an, verb is is/was

newsadviceinformationfurnituremoneyhairluggageknowledgeprogressresearchworkweather

The news is good. · a piece of advice

Only plural — no singular

verb is are/were; count with a pair of

scissorstrousersjeansshortsglassespyjamasclothesgoods

My jeans are new. · a pair of scissors

⚠️ Look plural, take a singular idea; look singular, take a plural verb → mathematics / physics / news = is. But police / people / cattle = are (The police are here).

Part 2 · Verbs

Irregular & state verbs ⚡

The confusing verb families — and the verbs that refuse the -ing form.

10 Verbs · Irregular forms

The verbs that trip everyone up

No change — V1 = V2 = V3

  • cost · cut · put · hurt · let · set
  • hit · shut · spread · cost · read (read /red/ in past)

Confusing look-alikes

  • lie (lay–lain) = лежать · lie (lied–lied) = лгать
  • lay (laid–laid) = класть (needs an object)
  • rise (rose–risen) вставать · raise (raised) поднимать
  • find (found) находить · found (founded) основать
  • hang (hung) вешать · hang (hanged) казнить
⚠️ Two of these do NOT change → Remember read keeps its spelling (only sound changes), and cost/cut/put look identical in all three forms — so “Yesterday it cost £5”, not costed.

12 Verbs · State verbs

Verbs that hate the -ing form

State verbs describe a state, not an action, so they stay Simple — I know ✅ / I am knowing ❌.

Thinking

knowbelieveunderstandremembermean

Feelings

likelovehatewantneedprefer

Having / being

ownbelongcontainseemconsist
⚠️ Meaning-change exceptions → some go continuous when they mean an action:
I think it's good (opinion) · I'm thinking about it (process)  |  She has a car (own) · She's having lunch (eating)  |  He is rude (always) · He's being rude (right now)  |  I see (understand) · I'm seeing the doctor (meeting)

Part 3 · Articles & comparison

a · an · the · — 🅰️

The article rules that go by sound, plus the geography that always takes the.

15 Articles · a vs an

It's about the SOUND, not the letter

Use an before a vowel sound, a before a consonant sound — listen, don't just look at the spelling.

Silent letter → an

  • an hour · an honest man
  • an honour · an heir
  • h is silent → vowel sound

“You” sound → a

  • a university · a uniform
  • a European · a euro
  • a one-way street /w/ sound
⚠️ Abbreviations follow the letter's sound → an MP, an FBI agent, an X-ray, an SOS (“em, ef, ex, es…” start with vowel sounds) — but a UFO, a NATO base (“you-fo”).

17 Articles · the vs no article

Geography & the zero article

Use “the”

  • Plural / union countries: the USA, the UK, the Netherlands
  • Rivers, seas, oceans: the Nile, the Atlantic
  • Mountain ranges: the Alps, the Urals
  • Deserts & island groups: the Sahara, the Bahamas

Use NO article (—)

  • Countries, cities, continents: Russia, London, Africa
  • Single mountains & lakes: (Mount) Everest, Lake Baikal
  • Streets: Oxford Street
  • Meals, languages, sports: have breakfast, speak English, play tennis
⚠️ “Institution” phrases drop the article → go to — bed / — school / — work / — hospital  and  travel by — car / bus / train. Compare: go to the school = the building (as a visitor).

19 Comparison · Irregulars

Comparatives that don't follow -er

Fully irregular

  • good → better → best
  • bad → worse → worst
  • little → less → least
  • much/many → more → most
  • far → farther/further → farthest/furthest

Special patterns

  • The more you read, the better you write.
  • It's getting colder and colder.
  • as tall as · not as/so tall as
  • elder / eldest = family only (my elder brother)
⚠️ Never double up → more easier, more better, most cleverest are wrong. One comparison marker only: easier, better, the cleverest.

Part 4 · Spelling & word building

Spelling, prefixes & false friends ✍️

22 Spelling · Rules & exceptions

Spelling changes when you add endings

The rules

  • Double the final consonant (short stressed CVC): stop → stopping, begin → beginning
  • Drop silent -e before a vowel ending: make → making, hope → hoping
  • y → ies after a consonant: city → cities, study → studies
  • ie → y before -ing: die → dying, lie → lying

i before e…

“i before e, except after c”

  • believe, achieve, field
  • receive, deceive, ceiling (after c)
⚠️ Watch these → keep y after a vowel: play → played, boy → boys (not plaied).   -ful has one L: careful, helpful.   And the i-before-e exceptions: seize, weird, protein, foreign, height, either, science.

24 Word building · Negative prefixes

Which negative prefix?

The prefix usually depends on the first letter of the word.

im- (before m, p)

impossibleimpatientimmature

il- (before l)

illegalillogicalilliterate

ir- (before r)

irregularirresponsibleirrelevant

in- (default)

incorrectinvisibleinformal

un- / dis- / mis-

unhappydishonestmisunderstand

-able vs -ible

comfortablereliablepossiblevisible
⚠️ Adverb traps → good → well (not goodly), fast → fast, hard → hard. And beware hard (усердно) vs hardly (едва — почти не!).

26 Vocabulary · Traps

Confusables & false friends

English homophones

  • its (his) · it's (it is)
  • their / there / they're
  • than (comparison) · then (time)
  • affect (verb) · effect (noun)
  • lose (терять) · loose (свободный)
  • advice (noun) · advise (verb)

Russian «ложные друзья»

  • magazine = журнал (shop = store)
  • sympathetic = сочувствующий (nice = симпатичный)
  • actually = на самом деле (≠ актуально)
  • accurate = точный (neat = аккуратный)
  • fabric = ткань (factory = фабрика)
  • intelligent = умный (cultured = интеллигентный)
⚠️ A few more classics → brilliant = блестящий (not бриллиант = diamond) · receipt = чек (not рецепт = recipe) · decade = десятилетие · sensible (разумный) vs sensitive (чувствительный).

Part 5 · Collective nouns

Groups of animals 🐾

a flock of birds, a herd of cows, a pride of lions — English has a special word for almost every group (собирательные существительные).

· Collective nouns · Animals

Words for groups of animals

The main ones

  • a herd of — cows, cattle (стадо), elephants, deer
  • a herd / drove of horses — табун
  • a flock of — sheep (отара), goats, birds
  • a pack of — wolves, dogs, hounds (свора, стая волков)
  • a pride of lions — прайд
  • a swarm of — bees, insects (рой)

Water & small animals

  • a school / shoal of fish — косяк
  • a pod of — whales, dolphins
  • a colony of — ants, bats, rabbits
  • a troop of monkeys
  • a litter of — puppies, kittens, cubs (помёт)
  • a nest of — mice, snakes
⚠️ Russian → English map → стадо = herd · табун = herd/drove of horses · отара = flock of sheep · свора = pack · прайд = pride · рой = swarm · косяк = school/shoal · помёт = litter · стая (волков) = pack, стая (птиц) = flock.
· Collective nouns · Birds

Birds — a different word for each! 🐦

The general word is a flock of birds, but many birds have their own special (and funny) collective noun:

Wild birds

  • a murder of crows 🐦‍⬛
  • a parliament of owls 🦉
  • an unkindness of ravens
  • a murmuration of starlings
  • an exaltation of larks
  • a charm of goldfinches

On the ground / water

  • a gaggle of geese (on land) 🪿
  • a skein of geese (in flight)
  • a colony of penguins / gulls 🐧
  • a brood of chickens / hens 🐤 (выводок)
  • a covey of partridges / quail
⚠️ These are "terms of venery" → English has 100+ of them and olympiads love the funny ones — especially a murder of crows and a parliament of owls. Learn the odd ones by heart.
