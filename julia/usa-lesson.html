05 Discover · The basics

USA — the fast facts

Country & state

  • Full name: the United States of America (the USA / the US / the States)
  • Capital: Washington, D.C. · Currency: the US dollar ($)
  • 50 states + the federal district of Washington, D.C.
  • Population: about 335 million (3rd largest in the world)
  • Language: English — but there is no official language in law

Symbols & history

  • Flag: the Stars and Stripes — 50 stars (states) + 13 stripes (first colonies)
  • First president: George Washington
  • Independence Day: 4 July 1776 (from Britain)
  • National bird: the bald eagle 🦅
  • Motto: "In God We Trust" · nickname: "the Land of the Free"
⚠️ Classic olympiad trap → the biggest city is New York, but the capital is Washington, D.C. And D.C. (the District of Columbia) is not a state!
06 Discover · Territory

A country the size of a continent 🌎

Size & position

  • One of the largest countries on Earth (about 9.8 million km²)
  • Washed by two oceans: the Atlantic (east) and the Pacific (west)
  • Neighbours: Canada to the north, Mexico to the south
  • Alaska and Hawaii are separated from the other 48 states
  • So wide it has 6 time zones — when New York wakes up, Los Angeles is still asleep

Four regions

  • The Northeast — old cities: New York, Boston, Washington
  • The South — warm & sunny: Florida, Texas, Georgia
  • The Midwest — flat farmland & the Great Lakes: Chicago
  • The West — mountains, deserts & the Pacific: California
⚠️ Don't mix them up → Alaska = the largest state (area), but California = the most populous (most people). The 48 joined states are called the "lower 48" or contiguous states.
07 Discover · Nature

Nature & famous landscapes 🏔️

Mountains & canyons

  • The Rocky Mountains — the great range of the West
  • The Appalachians — older mountains in the East
  • The Grand Canyon (Arizona) — a natural wonder
  • Denali (Alaska) — the highest peak in North America

Water

  • The Mississippi & Missouri — the longest rivers
  • The Great Lakes — five huge lakes shared with Canada
  • Niagara Falls — famous waterfalls on the border

Extremes & parks

  • Yellowstone — the first national park (1872), geysers & bison
  • Death Valley — the hottest, driest, lowest place
  • The Mojave & other deserts of the Southwest
  • The Everglades (Florida) — wetlands full of alligators

🌡️ From frozen Alaska to tropical Hawaii and hot Florida, the USA has almost every climate on Earth.

09 Discover · Wildlife

American animals 🐻🦅

National symbols

  • 🦅 Bald eagle — the national bird (on the coat of arms)
  • 🦬 American bison (buffalo) — the national mammal

The wild West & North

  • 🐻 Grizzly bear & black bear
  • 🫎 Moose and elk in the northern forests
  • 🐺 Coyote, wolf and bobcat

The warm South

  • 🐊 Alligator — in Florida's swamps
  • 🐍 Rattlesnake — a desert snake with a warning "rattle"
  • 🦝 Raccoon — the clever "masked" city animal
⚠️ Careful → the plural of bison is bison (no -s!), like deer → deer and fish → fish. And a buffalo and a bison are the same animal in American English.

13 Discover · Symbols & culture

Symbols everyone should know 🗽

Monuments

  • 🗽 Statue of Liberty (New York) — a gift from France in 1886
  • 🗿 Mount Rushmore (S. Dakota) — 4 presidents in rock
  • 🏛️ The White House — the president's home in D.C.
  • 🌉 Golden Gate Bridge (San Francisco)

Pop culture

  • 🎬 Hollywood — films & movie stars (Los Angeles)
  • 🎶 Jazz, blues, country, hip-hop, rock 'n' roll
  • 🍔 Fast food: burgers, hot dogs, donuts, apple pie
  • 🛣️ Route 66 — the famous cross-country road

Sport & holidays

  • 🏈 American football · ⚾ baseball · 🏀 basketball
  • 🦃 Thanksgiving (4th Thursday of November)
  • 🎃 Halloween (31 October)
  • 🎆 Independence Day (4 July) — fireworks!
⚠️ Famous facts to remember → the Statue of Liberty was a gift from France; Mount Rushmore shows Washington, Jefferson, Lincoln and Roosevelt; the president lives in the White House in Washington, D.C.

17 Task · Reading (True / False / Not stated)

Read, then decide

New York City

New York City, often called "the Big Apple", is the largest city in the United States, with more than eight million people. It stands on the Atlantic coast, mostly on a group of islands, and is famous all over the world for its tall skyscrapers, yellow taxis and bright lights.

The city is made up of five parts, called boroughs: Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, the Bronx and Staten Island. Manhattan is the busy heart of the city, where you can find Times Square, Central Park and Wall Street, the centre of American business.

Millions of tourists visit every year. Many of them take a boat to see the Statue of Liberty, which was a gift from France in 1886. Others walk through Central Park, a huge green space in the middle of the concrete city, or watch a show on Broadway. Although New York is not the capital of the country, it is often seen as its cultural capital — a place where people from every corner of the world live side by side.

  • 1. New York City is the largest city in the USA.
  • 2. New York City is the capital of the USA.
  • 3. The city is built only on the mainland, not on islands.
  • 4. The Statue of Liberty was a present from France.
  • 5. Tickets for Broadway shows are expensive.
  • 6. New York City has five boroughs.
